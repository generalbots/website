Capabilities

Everything a Modern Tasks Needs

Powerful capabilities with AI orchestration — all self-hosted on your infrastructure.

Natural Language App Creation

Describe a CRM, inventory system, or tracker in plain English and Tasks generates the tables, UI, and automation with a confidence score and ETA.

Intent Classification

Requests are auto-routed: app creation, to-dos, monitors, immediate actions, schedulers, goals, and voice tools each get the right handler.

Real-Time Progress Tracking

Watch every step execute with a live progress panel and terminal-style log showing running, pending, completed, failed, and attention states.

Execution Modes

Run plans fully automatic, supervised with per-step approval, or dry-run to preview exactly what would happen before anything executes.

Schedulers & Monitors

Generate recurring scripts (e.g. a summary every Monday at 8am) and change monitors that fire when stock drops below a threshold.

Task Controls & API

Execute, pause, resume, cancel, retry, or modify any plan from the UI or via REST endpoints, including generated HTMX apps.
