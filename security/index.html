02Report a vulnerability

Send a private report before disclosing publicly. Include affected versions, reproduction steps and impact.

  • Email security@pragmatismo.com.br. Ask for the current PGP fingerprint in your first message if the issue is sensitive.
  • We acknowledge within 3 business days and triage within 10.
  • Coordinated disclosure: 90-day embargo, extendable by agreement.

No bug bounty program

Reward budget: 0. We offer public credit only — in the hall of fame and in advisory credits.
