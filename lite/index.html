Choose your model

Use a local model or connect a hosted provider. When you choose a hosted model, your prompts go to that provider.

Pick your workspace

Choose the folder where Lite stores your conversations, files, and workspace database.

A few things to know

Can I use it offline?

Once a local model and the required components are installed, local inference can run offline. Hosted models and connected services require a connection.

Where is my workspace stored?

Lite stores your workspace files and database on your computer, in the folder you choose during setup.
